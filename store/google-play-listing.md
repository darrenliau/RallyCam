# Google Play listing (draft)

**Before submitting to Play, read this.** The live overlay in the video and the native camera exist only on iPhone so far. On Android the app uses the older camera and the overlay is not drawn into the video, and I have never run an Android build. Launch on iPhone first. Do the Android work and a real device test before using this page.

## App access
No login needed. All functionality is available without special access.

## Closed testing requirement
Personal developer accounts created recently must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before applying for production. Plan for about three weeks. Organisation accounts are exempt. Check the current rule in Play Console before relying on this.

## Assets
- Icon 512 x 512 (use `assets/icon.png` scaled), feature graphic 1024 x 500, at least 2 phone screenshots (landscape or portrait, 16:9 or 9:16 between 320 and 3840 px).
