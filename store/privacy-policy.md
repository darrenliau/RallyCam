# RallyCam privacy policy

_Last updated: DATE. Replace DATE and the contact line before publishing this at a public web address (for example GitHub Pages), because both stores ask for a link._

RallyCam records badminton games with your phone's camera and shows the score and player statistics. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version is that everything stays on your phone.

## What RallyCam uses
- **Camera.** To film your game and to find the players on the court. The pictures are processed on your phone.
- **Microphone.** To record the sound of your game along with the video.
- **What you type.** Player names, venue names and the scores you enter.

## Where it is stored
Videos, scores, player names, venue names and court positions are stored only on your phone, inside the app. RallyCam has no accounts and does not send any of this to us or to anyone else.

## What RallyCam does not do
- It does not collect personal data on a server.
- It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
- It does not share your videos with anyone. A video leaves your phone only when you choose to share it yourself, for example with AirDrop or by saving it to Photos.

## People in your videos
Your videos can show other people, such as your opponent or people nearby. Please ask them before you share a video. If you share it, what happens after that is up to you and the service you share it with.

## Children
RallyCam is not aimed at children under 13 and does not ask for any personal information.

## Deleting your data
Delete a game inside the app to remove its video and score. Deleting the app removes everything RallyCam stored on your phone.
