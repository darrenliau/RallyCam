## Description
RallyCam turns your phone into a scoreboard camera for badminton.

Put your phone on a tripod behind the court, line up the court corners once, and start. RallyCam films your game in landscape and puts the scoreboard and player tags straight into the video, so the video you share already shows who is winning.

WHAT YOU GET
- A scoreboard that follows the real rules: 21 points, win by 2, capped at 30, with the server and serving court
- Names for singles or doubles players, and for each venue you play at
- Player tags and a court map in the video, with distance covered
- The game stops recording by itself when someone wins
- Fix a wrong point at any time
- A review screen with the video, a score chart, and every point
- Court positions are remembered for each venue
- Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking

GOOD TO KNOW
- Use a tripod behind the near baseline, with the whole court in view
- You tap the winner after each point. Automatic scoring from the shuttle is not part of this version
- Works best on iPhones with an ultra-wide camera

## App Review notes
RallyCam needs camera and microphone access to film a game. No sign-in is needed. To test: open the app, tap Record, enter two names, tap Start game, allow camera and microphone, drag the four dots onto a rectangle (any four corners work for a test), tap Lock court and start recording, tap the "+" buttons to score points, then End game. The finished video opens with the overlay in it. A badminton court is not needed to try it.
